INT. SMALL KITCHEN - NIGHT

MAYA, forties, sits alone at a table with a cold cup of tea. The clock on the wall reads 2:14. She stares at a phone lying face-down in front of her.

She turns the phone over. A message from her brother: "Are you awake?" She types a reply, deletes it, types again.

EXT. APARTMENT ROOFTOP - CONTINUOUS

Maya steps out into the cold air. The city hums far below. She sits on the low wall at the edge and lets her legs hang over the side.

Behind her, the door opens. Her brother DEV stands in the doorway, out of breath.
